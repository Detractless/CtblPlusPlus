Account Setup

A second account is needed

The next step creates a second administrator account. Here's why that's needed.

Why a second account?

CTBL++ can't protect against the account running it. If your main account is an administrator, it has enough access to override the enforcement — kill services, modify files, uninstall. That's not a hard seal.

There's also a specific Windows behavior: if the account that registered the services gets demoted to standard user, the services stop. So we need a dedicated account that stays admin and never gets touched.

The following step creates that account. After install, you'll discard its password and demote your own — leaving no usable administrator access on this machine.
